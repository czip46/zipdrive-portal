      Common questions
      
Is my vehicle insured while you drive it?
Yes. ZIPDRIVE carries $1M primary commercial auto liability through Berkshire Hathaway. Our coverage responds first; your policy stays in excess. Pre-existing condition is photographed before every move.

      
Can I get a Certificate of Insurance?
Yes. Business clients can also be named as Additional Insured. Ask at 855-ZIP-OHIO.

      
How much does it cost?
Flat rate per move, based on route and vehicle. Residential moves in the Columbus area start at $65. Business and fleet moves are quoted by route. No meters and no surprise fees.

      
Do I need a contract?
No. ZIPDRIVE works per move with no retainers or monthly minimums. Recurring clients can set up a standing service agreement.

      
Can you do same-day?
In most cases, yes, subject to driver availability. Call or text early in the day. Airport returns and service appointments are best booked the day before.

      
What if there's an incident?
Our commercial policy handles claims from our drivers' operation of the vehicle. We notify you in writing within 24 hours and work with our carrier to resolve it.

      
What are your hours?
Monday – Friday, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM. Saturday for select services. Ask about after-hours requests.
